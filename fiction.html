Fiction and art

Invented companies, invented assets, and the stories that generate them

Everything on this page is made up. That is not a disclaimer bolted onto a marketing page — it is the reason the page exists, and the reason it is kept separate from every other page on this site.

Why this is here

A fixture with a name is either declared invented or it reads as a client

You cannot build a detector without generating the shape it is meant to catch, and you cannot generate that shape against a live record. So the controls in this system are exercised against fixtures — and a fixture that is only CLIENT-A cannot carry a premise, a history, or a set of books that disagree with each other.

The moment a fixture gets a name it starts to read as a real counterparty on a public page. The site build refuses a page carrying a denied term for exactly that reason. This section is the other half of that rule: the invented names live here, together, labelled.

The art is not decoration either. A deterministic renderer that draws the same tablet from the same digest is a verification tool that happens to be beautiful, and a story about a shortage is the cheapest way to find out whether a control still reads as fair when it lands on somebody sympathetic.

Standing declaration

No company, person, lot, bunker, meter or purchase order named on this page exists. No figure on it is a production figure. Nothing here describes a client, an engagement, or a real record — and where a mechanism is real, it is described on the onboarding and evidence pages instead, in its own terms.
